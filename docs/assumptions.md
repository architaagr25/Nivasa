# Assumptions

Everything here is not stated in the problem statement. Each is numbered so code comments and the TDD can refer to it.

| #    | Assumption                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Why                                                                                     |
| ---- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A-01 | "Nivasa" is the product name. The client stays "Company A" and its projects "Project 1" and "Project 2", as in the brief.                                                                                                                                                                                        | The rules say to use only the information in the problem statement.                     |
| A-02 | Project details (locality, unit mix, sizes, possession dates, amenities, coordinates) and RERA numbers are placeholders. RERA numbers contain `DEMO` so they cannot be mistaken for real registrations.                                                                                                          | Needed to make the fact sheet and RERA display work. The client supplies the real ones. |
| A-03 | All people, phone numbers and conversations in the seed data are fictional. Phone numbers use the 70000 xxxxx range.                                                                                                                                                                                             | No real personal data in the repository.                                                |
| A-04 | Seed volume matches the brief: about 100 leads a day (3,000 a month) for the last 60 days, so the dashboard numbers are at real scale.                                                                                                                                                                           | A dashboard of 20 rows proves nothing about cost per visit.                             |
| A-05 | Monthly spend of ₹30 lakh is split by source: portals ₹12 L, social ads ₹13.5 L, website (search ads) ₹3 L, hoarding ₹1.5 L.                                                                                                                                                                                     | The brief gives only the total. Needed for cost per visit by source.                    |
| A-06 | Seeded history before 19 Oct 2026 reflects the brief's "today" (median first response about 14 hours, 35% not reached in 48 hours). Leads from 19 Oct onward are answered by Nivasa. The seed data is synthetic.                                                                                                 | Lets the dashboard show the baseline and the change it is meant to measure.             |
| A-07 | Portal and ad lead forms include a consent checkbox, so those leads arrive with consent to be contacted about their enquiry on WhatsApp or SMS. Hoarding callers give consent verbally on the call, logged by staff.                                                                                             | DPDP: contact only with consent. Without this, no automatic first reply could be sent.  |
| A-08 | Site visits run 10:00–18:00 IST with a lunch break (slots at 10, 11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 17). Capacity is 2 visits per slot on weekdays and 4 on weekends, never more than the executives on that project.                                                                                                           | Real free slots need a definition of supply.                                            |
| A-09 | Sites are closed 8–10 Nov 2026 for Diwali and the Gujarati New Year (seeded as blackout dates).                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Shows blackout handling on realistic dates.                                             |
| A-10 | 11 sales executives: 6 on Project 1, 5 on Project 2. One sales head.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | The brief says 11 in sales.                                                             |
| A-11 | Buyers sign in with a mobile number and a one-time code. Delivery of codes and messages is simulated in an in-app outbox.                                                                                                                                                                                        | The brief says to mock messages and outside systems.                                    |
| A-12 | A mobile number is accepted if it is an Indian number of 10 digits starting 6–9. Some Ahmedabad landlines (079…) pass this check; that is acceptable because a landline caller is still a lead.                                                                                                                  | Avoids shipping the full phone-number database to slow phones.                          |
| A-13 | The free Gemini tier is used (Flash Lite models, about 500 requests a day each). Its terms allow Google to use the prompts; only synthetic data is sent.                                                                                                                                                         | The build must cost nothing to run.                                                     |
| A-14 | The brief doesn't say which language each sample lead uses. Nidhi Shah (LD-58211) is seeded as a Gujarati speaker who asked for a person, so the handoff shows a chat that isn't in English; Hardik Patel (LD-58210) holds the booked 1 Nov visit and doubles as the buyer demo account.                         | Shows the language and handoff features on the brief's own sample data.                 |
| A-15 | Visiting times are kept 8 weeks ahead, and the assistant offers those in the next 14 days. Online booking closes 3 hours before a visit.                                                                                                                                                                         | Gives the sales team time to prepare and the 2-hour reminder time to go out.            |
| A-16 | A buyer can hold one upcoming visit per project. Booking again offers to move the existing visit instead. An executive is never booked for two visits at the same time.                                                                                                                                          | Stops accidental double bookings from repeat taps or a second tab.                      |
| A-17 | If a reminder's time has already passed when the visit is booked, or when the job runs, it is recorded as skipped, not sent late.                                                                                                                                                                                | A "see you tomorrow" message an hour before the visit would be wrong.                   |
| A-18 | Lead score is rules, not AI. Hot: a visit is booked, or the budget covers the project's starting price, the buyer plans to buy within 6 months and has given a mobile number. Cold: budget more than 15% below the starting price, or buying in more than a year. Everything else with some information is warm. | The score decides who gets a salesperson's time, so it must be explainable.             |
| A-19 | A handoff goes to the executive who already owns the lead; otherwise to the executive on that project with the fewest open leads.                                                                                                                                                                                | Keeps one buyer with one person and spreads new work evenly.                            |
| A-20 | The same mobile number is the same buyer and is merged automatically. The same email with a different number, or a very similar name on the same project within 30 days, is only suggested as a duplicate; the sales head decides.                                                                               | Merging the wrong two buyers is worse than showing a possible duplicate.                |
| A-21 | A reply of STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, रोकें, बंद करें or બંધ કરો opts the number out of all automatic messages, including reminders. The buyer can opt back in from their visit pass.                                                                                                                                    | DPDP: honour opt-outs in the buyer's language.                                          |
| A-22 | "First response" is the first message the buyer receives, automatic or from staff. A hoarding call counts as answered when it was taken.                                                                                                                                                                         | The brief measures response time but does not define it.                                |
| A-23 | Cost per completed visit is the source's spend in the period divided by the visits that took place from leads that came in during that period. Monthly spend is spread evenly over the days and is not split by project.                                                                                         | The brief asks for cost per visit by source but gives spend only per month.             |
| A-24 | Executives see their own leads and unassigned ones; an unassigned lead's number is masked until they take it. The sales head sees all leads in full. Staff screens are in English only.                                                                                                                          | DPDP: show personal data only to the person who needs it.                               |
| A-25 | The first automatic reply and every reminder include the project's RERA number and only registered details. They are written as approved templates, ready to submit as WhatsApp templates later.                                                                                                                 | RERA and WhatsApp template rules in the brief.                                          |
| A-26 | Limits against abuse: 20 chat messages a minute and 10 new chats an hour from one visitor, 8 a minute and 60 an hour in one conversation, 5 sign-in codes an hour to one number, and 10 staff sign-in attempts per 5 minutes.                                                                                    | Protects buyers' phones and the free AI quota from scripts.                             |
| A-27 | In demo mode the sign-in page lists the demo staff accounts with their shared password and sign-in codes appear on screen, so anyone with the link can act as the sales head. A real deployment turns demo mode off.                                                                                             | Lets a reviewer try every role without setup.                                           |
